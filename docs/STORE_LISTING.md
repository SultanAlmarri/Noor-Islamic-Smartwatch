# Google Play listing for Minhad (draft, 22 November 2026)

Texts to copy into the Play Console (Grow → Store presence → Main store listing, then "Manage translations" for
Arabic). Written from the app as it is at 0.24.1 and from the measured evidence in `docs/PRAYER_ACCURACY.md` and
`accuracy/results/EVIDENCE.md`. Lengths were counted by script and are inside Play's limits as the author knows
them (name 30 characters, short description 80, full description 4000); the Console counts again as you type.

**Rules these texts keep, and any later edit should keep:**

- No claim of accuracy beyond the evidence: no "most accurate", no "exact", no "official app".
- No claim that anything was tested on a particular phone or watch.
- Not affiliated with any authority, and saying so.
- No words Play's listing rules object to as far as known ("best", "#1", "free", prices, emoji in the name).
- **[DEPENDS]** marks a line that is true today but depends on a decision in `docs/RELEASE.md` (the Dubai
  recordings, Minhad Studio on the watch, background location). Remove or keep it to match what is released.

## 3. Full description (at most 4000 characters)

### English

```
Minhad shows the prayer times for where you are, calls the Adhan at each prayer, and points to the Qibla, on your Android phone and your Wear OS watch. The home screen is a sky: the Sun, Moon, stars and clouds move through the day over a natural landscape, and the prayer times sit along the Sun's path.

No account. No ads. No tracking. Prayer times are calculated on your device and work without the internet.

PRAYER TIMES
• For your location, or for any place you choose: search, browse by country, enter coordinates, or put a pin on the map. More than 800,000 places are built in and work offline.
• The method follows the place: Minhad picks the prayer-time authority or method used in each country, and you can choose another, set the Asr school, a high-latitude rule and your own minute adjustments.
• The app says where each day's times come from: an authority's published timetable, a calculation checked against one, or a widely used method.
• Any date from 2021 to 2035, with the Hijri and Gregorian calendars side by side.

HOW CLOSE ARE THE TIMES?
We measure instead of promising. Minhad's calculation was compared by computer with timetables that authorities publish. For Dubai, 527 of 588 published times matched to the minute and the other 61 differed by one minute. For Singapore, the published timetable for 2024–2026 is carried inside the app and shown as published. For some countries the method has not yet been compared with an official timetable, and the app says so. Your mosque's times may differ by a minute or two. Minhad is an independent app and is not affiliated with any religious authority or government body.

ADHAN AND ALERTS
• The Adhan at each prayer, like an alarm: it can sound with the phone locked or on silent, with a Stop button.
• Voices recorded in Makkah, Madinah and Cairo, each whole or as its opening, or a recording of your own.
• For each prayer: on or off, Adhan, short Adhan, a nature sound, a notification, or silent; volume and vibration.
• A reminder before each prayer, and your own alarms before or after any prayer, with Snooze.
• An alert history that tells you what happened to every alert, so you know the Adhan was called.

QIBLA
A compass to the Kaaba with the bearing, the compass's own accuracy, and help with calibration.

ON YOUR HOME SCREEN AND STATUS BAR
• Six widgets you can restyle: next prayer, today's prayers, the sky, a countdown ring, a prayer strip and the Hijri date.
• A countdown to the next prayer in your notifications, if you want it.

ON YOUR WEAR OS WATCH
• Next prayer and a live countdown, today's times, the Qibla, a tasbih counter and the Hijri calendar.
• Two tiles and four complications for your watch face.
• Prayer alerts on the watch, the phone, or both.
• Minhad watch faces in three designs and five colours (Wear OS 6 and later).
• Works with the phone app or on its own: the watch can be set up without a phone.

LANGUAGES
English and Arabic, right to left, with Western or Arabic-Indic digits.

PRIVACY
Your location is used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla. Minhad's publisher receives nothing: there is no account and no server of ours. The privacy policy lists the few moments when something leaves your phone, for example when you open the map.

Minhad needs Android 8 or later. The watch app needs Wear OS 4 or later.
```

**Honest pictures.** Play expects screenshots to show the app as users get it. The repository's rendered screens
(`app/build/screenshots`, CI's `screenshots.zip`) are software drawings: useful as a guide to which screens to
capture, but the home sky on a real phone is drawn by the phone's graphics chip and looks different (better). Take
the store pictures **on the owner's phone and watch** (or, for the watch, a Wear OS emulator through the
`watch-face.yml` workflow if the real watch cannot be captured). Do not add captions that promise more than the
description does.

### Phone: which screens to capture (in this order)

1. **Today, late afternoon or sunset**, a scenery the owner likes (Graphics: High), countdown to Maghrib visible,
   all six times on screen. This is the first picture people see.
2. **Today at night**: stars or the Milky Way, the Moon, countdown to Fajr. Shows that the sky changes.
3. **Adhan tab**: the next Adhan, the prayers with their switches.
4. **A prayer's alert page or the voices page**: the choice of voice and kind of alert.
5. **Qibla**: the compass aligned on the Kaaba.
6. **Widgets on a home screen**: two or three of Minhad's widgets placed on the phone's home screen.
7. **Location**: "Confirm the location" with the map, the place's name and today's times there.
8. **Watch page** (Settings → Watch): the watch connected and the watch-face chooser.

For the Arabic listing, the same eight with the app's language set to Arabic and Arabic-Indic digits.

### Wear OS: which screens to capture

1. **Home**: next prayer, live countdown, today's times.
2. **Qibla** on the watch.
