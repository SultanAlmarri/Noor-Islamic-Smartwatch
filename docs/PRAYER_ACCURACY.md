# Prayer-time accuracy

Status on 16 November 2026. Every figure here is produced by tests in this repository; none is estimated. The full
evidence, authority by authority, is `accuracy/results/EVIDENCE.md` (regenerated by `accuracy/tools/evidence.py`).

## Is 100% possible?

- **By calculation, no: not for IACAD, and not for any authority measured so far.** For IACAD it is impossible with
  any single rule: even Dhuhr, which depends only on longitude and the equation of time, cannot be reproduced on every
  official day by any calculation plus any fixed rounding and precaution minutes (the official minus calculated
  differences spread over 65 s, more than one minute; simpler ephemerides do no better). Details below.
- **By the authority's own table, yes, by construction**, on the dates the app carries it: IACAD 97 official days
  (2021–2023, November 2026), MUIS Singapore 2024–2026. A runtime source exists for Awqaf (an official API that needs
  a key the owner must register for); none that the app may use exists yet for IACAD (see "Routes to exact times").

## Dubai — IACAD (Dulook DXB)

The app's Dubai profile is research candidate **C1**:

| Element | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Reference point (IACAD's "Dubai City") | 25.12° N, 55.189° E | Deduced from official data (latitude ±0.03°; longitude is an equivalent value, see below) |
| Fajr / Isha | Sun 18.0° below the horizon | Deduced; 18.0° fits 2017–2020 exactly, current era fits 17.4–18.2° |
| Sunrise / Maghrib horizon | Sun's radius + 34′ refraction + 0.10° | Deduced |
| Dhuhr | Meridian transit | Deduced (96 of 98 days fit) |
| Asr | One shadow length | Deduced |
| Rounding | Seconds rounded up to the minute | Equivalent to round-to-nearest at 55.31° E with one more minute on every prayer; the two give identical times |
| Precaution minutes | Fajr −2, Shuruq −4, Dhuhr +2, Asr 0, Maghrib +2, Isha 0 | Recovered unchanged from 2020–2023 alone and from November 2026 alone |

**Result against 588 official IACAD times (98 days, 20 Aug 2020 – 30 Nov 2026): 527 exact (89.6%); all 61 others
differ by exactly 1 minute.** By prayer: Fajr 82/98, Shuruq 90, Dhuhr 91, Asr 91, Maghrib 87, Isha 86. The test
`PrayerEngineTest.iacadOfficialAgreementIsAsDocumented` locks these numbers, and every mismatch is listed in
`accuracy/results/discrepancies_candidate_C1__lat_25_12__E3.csv`.

**Why the rest cannot be reached by calculation (16 Nov 2026, `accuracy/results/EVIDENCE.md`).** A table made by
"one smooth calculation + one fixed rule" leaves official-minus-calculated differences inside one 60-second window.
IACAD's do not, on any prayer: Fajr 86 s, Shuruq 85, Dhuhr 65, Asr 67, Maghrib 62, Isha 74. The best possible fixed
shift per prayer, fitted to the data (an upper bound, not a usable rule), would reach 550/588. Tested and rejected
(`accuracy/tools/ephemeris_variants.py`): the USNO and Meeus low-precision Sun (at the event, at noon, at 0 h UT),
Spencer's equation of time, and perpetual tables copied from one base year (2016–2027): best Dhuhr spread 62 s,
best ceiling 554/588. Richer rule sets refitted from scratch (`accuracy/tools/iacad_refit.py`) fit their own days
better (up to 544/588) but do worse than C1 on days they never saw (293/366 vs 300/366; 451/546 vs 470/546), so C1
is kept. IACAD's tables also show manual edits in earlier years. No date-specific adjustment is used.

**More days checked.** The official Dulook DXB app (owner's screen recording, 7 days outside the November feed):
39/42. Emirates 24|7's copy of IACAD's daily times, recovered from Internet Archive captures (62 days, May 2023 –
March 2026; secondary, validation only): 305/372 (82.0%), all others 1 minute. Locked by `OfficialAgreementTest`.

IACAD changed conventions with the 1439 AH calendar (21 Sep 2017) and the 1442 AH calendar (20 Aug 2020); only data
from 20 Aug 2020 validates the current profile. Details: `accuracy/README.md`.

### Engine verification

The Kotlin engine (`core/prayer`) is a port of the NREL Solar Position Algorithm. Against the research reference
(pvlib) it agrees to **0.007 s** on all 37,254 Dubai event times 2014–2030 and to 0.005 s on 1,560 times for Makkah,
Cairo, New York, Jakarta and Karachi (`PrayerEngineTest`, also runnable without Gradle via `tools/verify/LocalVerify.kt`).

## Hijri date

The app uses the Umm al-Qura calendar built into Java, with a ±2-day user adjustment. It can differ from the UAE
calendar by a day: on 4 November 2026 Umm al-Qura gives 24 Jumada al-Ula 1448, IACAD's feed gives 23.

## UAE — Awqaf (outside Dubai)

Replaced on 16 November 2026 from evidence (`accuracy/tools/awqaf_fit.py`):

| | Old provisional profile | New profile |

| Awqaf's own website, Abu Dhabi region, 16–17 Nov 2026 (never used in the fit) | 8/12 | **12/12** |

New conventions: Fajr/Isha 18.1°, horizon = Sun's radius + 34′ + 0.31°, rounded to the nearest minute except Sunrise
and Isha (rounded up), minutes −1, −3, +2, 0, +1, −1, reference point 24.44 N 54.28 E for users within 60 km of
Abu Dhabi (18.1° and the point are equivalent fitted values, not published ones). Other regions are calculated at the
user's position and have not been compared with an official table. Locked by
`OfficialAgreementTest.awqafProfileAgainstTheUaeCalendarForAbuDhabi` and `awqafProfileAgainstAwqafsWebsite`.

| Authority | Route | Status |
|---|---|---|
| IACAD | Website feed (iacad.gov.ae/en/prayer-times) | Refuses servers (Cloudflare, HTTP 403, checked again 16 Nov 2026); no published API or licence. Needs IACAD's permission before a phone fetches it. New months can be carried from a browser visit. |
