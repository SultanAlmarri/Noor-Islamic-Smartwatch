## Per prayer, and the ceiling of any calculation

"Ceiling" is the most days that ANY rule "the app's model time + one constant shift per prayer, then rounded" could
get right (it covers every rounding rule and every precaution minute, and is an upper bound: such a shift is
fitted to the data). "Spread" is the width of the official minus model residuals; over 60 s, no constant rule can
be exact on every day, whatever its constants.

"Rule at the ceiling": official = the model time plus c seconds, rounded up, for any c in the range shown
(a range that contains a whole minute k means "round up, then add k minutes" reaches the ceiling).
